VSR for Defence, Railway & Aerospace

Dimensional Stability for Strategic, Transportation & Precision Engineering Applications

Strategic Infrastructure Specs

Managing Residual Stresses in Strategic & Heavy-Duty Assets

Demanding cyclic loads, vibration, impact and torque can combine with residual stresses introduced by multi-pass welding, flame cutting, forming, fabrication and machining. Formula 62® VSR provides a controlled non-thermal option for suitable defence, railway and aerospace structures and precision tooling applications.

Connect on LinkedIn

Supporting Dimensional Stability in Demanding Service Conditions

VSR can be applied at the point of manufacture before final machining or assembly, helping support dimensional accuracy, alignment and reliable downstream manufacturing.

Engineering Quality Controls & Technical Oversight

Application review considers material, geometry, fabrication history, weight and dimensional requirements. Treatment planning covers equipment configuration, isolation, vibration parameters, resonance response and process documentation under experienced engineering oversight.

Aerospace Precision Tooling

For suitable aerospace manufacturing applications, Formula 62® VSR can be applied to large precision tooling structures to help manage residual stresses and support dimensional stability before subsequent precision manufacturing operations. GNSR has applied Formula 62® VSR to a large aluminium tooling structure used for aircraft- wing forming.

Heavy Defence, Railway & Aerospace Components Treated At-Site

Mobile Formula 62® systems can be deployed to customer facilities for large components, including components of 150+ tonnes subject to characteristics, application requirements and equipment configuration, helping reduce the need for transportation to external treatment facilities.
