Dimensional Stability for Heavy Rotating & Fabricated Components

Request Sugar Mill VSR Quote

Stabilize Heavy Components Before Precision Machining

GNSR brings Formula 62® At-Site VSR to sugar mills and heavy-industry facilities to help manage residual stresses and support dimensional stability in large rotating, fabricated and machined components.

Managing Residual Stresses in Critical Sugar-Mill Components

High mechanical loads, torque and cyclic service combine with residual stresses introduced by welding, repair, fabrication and machining. Application-specific VSR can help manage dimensional movement, alignment and stress- related issues in suitable components.

Specialized Sugar-Mill Component Consultation & Engineering Oversight

Treatment planning considers material, dimensions, weight, geometry, manufacturing stage and component function, including rollers, shafts, pinions, gears and heavy fabricated structures.
