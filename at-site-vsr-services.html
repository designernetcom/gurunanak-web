Technical Capacities

Relieve Residual Stress. Treat Components Where They Stand.

GNSR mobilizes Formula 62® VSR equipment and experienced field engineering support directly to customer facilities across India. The process applies controlled vibratory energy to help relieve and redistribute residual stresses generated by welding, flame cutting, forming, casting and rough machining-without furnace heating.

Why Residual Stress Matters

Residual stresses can contribute to dimensional movement, distortion and alignment issues during subsequent machining, fabrication or service. Application-specific VSR treatment helps manufacturers manage these stresses before critical finishing, assembly or operational stages.


The At-Site Advantage

Large or heavy components can be treated where they are manufactured or repaired, reducing the need for transportation to external furnace facilities and helping improve production flexibility.

Typical Treatment Cycle

Approximately 30–60 minutes per treatment zone, depending on component and application.
